Puppy Math

The breeder said every few hours, and the bladder keeps a stricter calendar than that.

Boundary: illustrative arithmetic only Puppy Math does schedule arithmetic with labeled care norms - months plus one hours between breaks, four-three-two meals as the months climb, about a cup a day per 10 pounds of expected adult weight. Your vet and the feeding chart on your own food bag win every tie. This page never gives a health verdict.

The potty truth

A puppy holds it about age in months plus one hours, capped at 8. A 3-month-old asks a break about every 4 hours - which is why the first month feels like shift work, because it is.

The meal truth

Under 3 months it is 4 meals a day, 3 to 5 months it is 3, and from 6 months it is 2. The bowl does not get bigger when the count drops - the portions do.

The kibble truth

About a cup a day per 10 pounds of expected adult weight. A puppy growing toward 40.0 pounds asks 4.0 cups a day, 1.3 cups a meal across 3 meals - and the bag math follows from there.
