Puppy Math

The food bag does the prescribing; this page only does the dividing.

Boundary: illustrative arithmetic only The potty line is a rough heuristic people repeat, and the feeding panels only divide the numbers on your food bag or from your vet. Your puppy, your label and your vet win every tie. This page never gives a care or health verdict.

The potty heuristic

A rough heuristic some trainers repeat says age in months plus one hours between breaks, capped at 8. It is a ceiling people cite, not a schedule - your puppy's signals and your vet set the real one.

The meal split

Your label's 3.0 cups a day split across 3 meals is 1.0 cup a meal. The label does the prescribing; the app only does the dividing.

The bag truth

Count the cups in the bag once, and at 3.0 cups a day a 100-cup bag covers 33.3 days. The bag stops surprising you.

The cost truth

A $58.00 bag at 3.0 cups a day runs $1.74 a day, about $52.20 a month. The bag size on the shelf is the marketing; this is the number.
